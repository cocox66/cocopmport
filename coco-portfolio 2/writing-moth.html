Moth

prose · Coco Xia

sometimes you thought you loved him; sometimes you fought to breathe in a maelstrom of emotions; you didn’t know if you’d plunged yourself into a paradise or an abyss; you wondered if it was even love that billowed within you.

your words never came out measured; they gushed; unbridled; unrestrained; the opposite of the Goleta ocean that never even broke into whitecaps; it was a flood that could drown both of you; though it would take him longer: he could make sins sound so heavenly; could destroy his own feelings the way an artist shatters unfinished ceramics; you knew that one day he would tell you that he couldn’t stay; but you still felt a stab when it happened; and so he kept you hanging like a shipwreck lost at sea; a canary with its wings clipped that sang a soulless song.
